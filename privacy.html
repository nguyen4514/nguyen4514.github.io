ThinkRead Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

ThinkRead operates strictly as a local-client processing utility. We do not host central infrastructure to collect, intercept, transmit, or monetize your parsing activities, browsing history, or reading logs.

Data Governance & External AI Providers

  • Execution Model: ThinkRead is an engine driven entirely by user-provided API credentials. We do not supply API keys, host inference models, or retain prompt/response data layers.
  • External Policies: Because processing runs through your own API architecture, all data interactions are governed strictly by the Service Level Agreements (SLAs), data retention parameters, model training restrictions, and privacy policies of your chosen AI infrastructure provider (e.g., Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq).
  • Permissions: Web structural extraction processes entirely within your local application sandbox. Tab permissions are utilized solely to isolate text contexts on your active display.

Commercial Tier & Licensing

  • ThinkRead features an optional paid tier. Premium license provisioning, subscription lifecycle management, and transaction execution are securely processed via Lemon Squeezy acting as our Merchant of Record.
  • Payment processing logs, licensing credentials, and customer emails are governed by Lemon Squeezy’s compliance infrastructure. No financial billing information ever enters or resides within the extension client data layer.

ThinkAuth Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

ThinkAuth manages web session states and application authentication boundaries exclusively inside secure, runtime-isolated client contexts.

Data Minimization & Local Execution

  • Operational Scope: ThinkAuth functions as a non-analytical local client workspace barrier. The utility alters localized layout appearances and intercepts sign-on endpoints strictly to enforce your chosen access rules without parsing deep code payloads or gathering telemetry.
  • Passive Design: ThinkAuth does not execute arbitrary script alterations, intercept transport payloads, or monitor user data traffic.
  • Token Isolation: Session configurations, target rules, and token boundaries remain locked within your browser's encrypted local data container. No credentials, tokens, or cryptographic hashes are shared with remote servers or third-party tracking scripts.

Commercial Tier & Licensing

  • ThinkAuth features a paid premium tier. Financial processing, customer verification, and active license validation arrays are handled entirely via Lemon Squeezy. No payment cards or financial transactions touch your extension's local background workers.

ThinkKB Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

ThinkKB facilitates on-demand content ingestion and browser documentation drafting loops solely under explicit end-user invocation.

Third-Party Infrastructure & Keys

  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): ThinkKB is an orchestration tool powered entirely by your personal AI provider keys. We do not collect, view, or retain your API credentials; keys are saved directly into your local client's sandboxed storage.
  • No Data Collection or Model Training: Because we do not control, lease, or host centralized models, Nguyen Labs never captures your documentation telemetry, reads your contexts, or uses your data for machine learning model training loops. You are bound entirely to the data protection rules of your explicit AI provider.
  • Secure OAuth Routing: Connections to external enterprise platforms (such as Google Workspace) utilize direct client-to-provider OAuth paths. No intermediate backend relays log, mirror, or cache your document assets.
