ThinkRead Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

ThinkRead operates strictly as a local-client processing utility. We do not host central infrastructure to collect, intercept, transmit, or monetize your parsing activities or reading logs.

All data structural extraction and sanitation operations process entirely within your application container sandbox. External network transmission only occurs if you intentionally configure specific personal API endpoint strings for LLM operations.

ThinkAuth Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

ThinkAuth manages web session states and application authentication boundaries exclusively inside secure, runtime-isolated contexts.

Credential & Token Handling

Session tokens remain stored strictly within your browser's encrypted local data layer. No credentials, tokens, or cryptographic hashes are shared with external analytics software or remote servers.

When connecting with external enterprise platform APIs (such as Google Workspace), data routing is handled directly through secure OAuth channels connecting your device client to the provider. No intermediate backend systems log or cache your documents.
